OUR story

Little Oven started in a small home kitchen in 2018, born from a love of French baking traditions and Filipino warmth. Our founder, Chef Amara, spent years training in Paris before bringing her craft back home.

Today, we bake hundreds of loaves and pastries every morning before sunrise — because great bread waits for no one. Every recipe is made with purpose, patience, and passion.
